Set in a fictional African country ravaged by civil war, the game offers players a vast, dynamic landscape filled with diverse ecosystems, day-night cycles, and unpredictable weather, enhancing the realism and immersion.

One of its standout features is the game's approach to realism. Players must manage resources like ammunition and health while dealing with the contamination that can affect weapons.
