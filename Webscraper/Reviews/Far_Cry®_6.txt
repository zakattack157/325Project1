Review Content: I have played all of the Far Cry games that have ever came out, by a large distant this is the worst one they have ever made. The story has some amazing parts but half of it is a complete mess and a huge waste of the players time and ultimately holds no part in any major decision. Half of the main "characters" could've had half their missions removed and still been as interesting, but alas we have to give all these lame n unoriginal characters a story because modern day representation has to exist.

Actual gameplay is actually one of the few good things, just like all other farcry games it holds great rewards for skilled players, guns are fun and all have their own use, being able to switch arsenal mid mission again is amazing and I actually like the difficulty from most the missions.

Enemy AI is the usual 50/50, sometimes its great and sometimes its trash. The usual Ubisoft stuff.
However, enemy control in this game is flawed, some places you just cannot take over no matter what you do because "uhh game said no" with no reasonable explanation.  The AIR control cannons are a bit of a joke, can clear them without actually even taking the base and all the special stuff you can get from it is mostly useless.

Vehicle control is the exact same as all other games, 0 complaints there.

Hunting has became absolutely pointless, same with fishing. There is no need to even consider doing either as there is no good reason or perks from doing so, just another time waste. Which is a shame because old games made those methods of gathering resources worth while.

The voice acting was amazing, super well done and I found 0 issues with anything audio related during the playthrough.

Over all, game just felt bland compared to old Farcry titles, most of the story felt forced, the boring inclusion of characters that had to be special and have "woke/dei" meanings behind them all felt super forced, lame and honestly for the south park reference... "GAY". 

4/10. Worst Farcry to date.

Let's hope 7 is better.
