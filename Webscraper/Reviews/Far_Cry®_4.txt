Review Content: FC4 is basically FC3 but:
- bigger
- like, much bigger, and more vertical
- more content and different side activities
- set in a mountainous forest instead of a tropical jungle.

It also works right out of the box here on PC unlike 3, which requires quite a bit of effort to get running well on Windows 11.

Anyways, the plot is definitely weaker than 3, and the gameplay doesn't evolve much past 3. However, the story missions and side content are a lot more fun, and mechanically, the game is more polished (gunplay, driving, graphics, etc.). Additionally, you gain access to the wingsuit early on here in 4, compared to the last third of 3.

I never played the multiplayer, so I can't comment on that. I enjoyed Hurk's DLC missions, but they didn't stand out in any way other than Hurk's bombastic personality. I didn't enjoy the prison escape DLC, mostly because it's designed as a replayable high score mode where you try to do as many things as possible in the time limit, which doesn't do it for me in a Far Cry game. I did like Valley of the Yetis though. It's short, and you can't replay the night survivals, but there's still fun to be had in my opinion.
