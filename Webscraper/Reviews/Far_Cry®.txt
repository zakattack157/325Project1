Review Content: Far Cry was the first video game I played, and it left a lasting impression. I vividly remember playing it on an old, underpowered PC during my 9th-grade years. My routine was to attend early morning Church communion classes, then hurry back home, eager to dive into the game. Those were truly memorable times.

The missions were challenging, and the creatures were genuinely terrifying. The game featured tough, imposing soldier enemies with that classic American, muscular look.

However, it was the background music that truly set the atmosphere, adding an eerie, suspenseful tone that permeated every scene. I’m grateful to Crytek for publishing such an exceptional game, and I hope the next Far Cry installment introduces non-human foes like the iconic Trigens once again.

Review ID: 179856317
Review Content: Great game! 

Kind of like a blend between Crysis and Half life.

One thing though: If you are playing this in 3440x1440 like I did, you may have huge problems actually hitting anything with your guns. Other people in videos on youtube seemed to hit the enemies from a long distance. I had to practically stand right beside them!

Then it hit me like a ton of bricks: This game was written in 2004, there were no screens in ultrawide back then. Maybe the targetting code in the game calculates the drift of bullets and targetting line from normal old screens with 4x3 aspect ratios and a lot lower resolutions that were common in 2004.

So i turned down the resolution to 1920 x 1080 and it feels like the targetting and hitting suddenly became a lot easier, problem solved :-)
