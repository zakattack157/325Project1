Review ID: 177039552
Review Content: It's a solid entry in the Far Cry franchise, even though in many aspects it's an almost carbon-copy from Far Cry 3. Noteworthy aspects are the locations and map, which features different scenery and beautiful vistas, complete with different fauna and flora, and multiple ways to explore the world. The gunplay is fun enough and the missions and activities are different enough to never be bored.

However, the main key negative aspect for me is the amount of repetitive tasks there is just to progress the story - liberating towers and outposts, collecting different pelts the same random encounter happening in a short period of time. 

Getting past that, it's a solid entry with a great story, and one of the most memorable villains in the saga.
