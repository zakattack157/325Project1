Review ID: 176145097
Review Content: Experience – First Time

World/Setting – 8.5/10
Story – 6/10
Music – 7.5/10
Gameplay – 9/10

Overall Review – 7.5/10
One Line Review – Hardest FPS Game

World/Setting
The World of Far Cry 1 is as diverse as you can imagine. I don’t want to spoil all the different settings, because it’s better to experience it firsthand. But to give you an idea: the game starts in a jungle, and ends on a volcano. That should be enough to tell you about the crazy places the game takes you in.

Story
The story is as generic as you can imagine. Think of a Chessy 80’s action sci-fi movie: you are 1 against 100s even 1000s of mercenaries. You start your journey on the premise, that you have to save a damsel in distress. But as you go deeper in to the jungle, the plot thickens, and pretty soon you are saving the World too!! The point is: don’t play the game for the story, play it for it’s game play.

Music
The music was great!! I loved the theme music. I loved all the background music that plays in the game. Although, sometimes it can over power what is happening on screen, sometimes! 

Gameplay
I loved it! 

Pros
The biggest pro for this 2004, almost 20 years old game is: the difficulty. I cannot remember the last time I played such a ‘challenging’ fps game. Every step you take: you will check your corners. You will scout; then plan your attack. You will die multiple times, sometimes in double figures, on a single mission!! But you will push forward, because you will love the challenge. And you need that dopamine hit. Dark Souls players know about this feeling. 

Cons
The game, sadly, is not without it’s flaws. The biggest one, in my opinion, was the stamina bar. Why… why do you need to bind jumping to stamina? And why does it deplete so fast? Aforementioned issues, you notice outright; all the other issues you start noticing as the game progresses like: AI. The AI in this game was hilarious at first: Shouting “Did you see something”, when I am not even close to them; shooting through tents; having literal aim bots. After some time, it starts to feel unfair, and frankly ‘annoying’. Some of the missions are outright broken like: the infamous ‘swamp car chase’, that you can’t even complete without an exploit!
